01 Client-Side WebAssembly Computer Vision

Luminix executes 100% of its MediaPipe machine learning inference locally inside your browser via WebAssembly (WASM) and WebGL acceleration. When you grant camera access for pose detection, 17 yoga asanas, or the adaptive gym state machine:

  • Raw camera frames are processed entirely in transient device RAM at 60 FPS.
  • No video stream, recorded clip, or photographic frame is ever transmitted to external servers.
  • Camera access terminates instantly the moment you navigate away, stop the workout, or close the browser tab.
  • Camera permissions are strictly scoped to (self) via HTTP Permissions-Policy headers.

02 Data We Collect & Storage Architecture

When you opt to create a Luminix account or personalize your biomechanical baseline, we store only the minimal necessary telemetry:

  • Identity Information: Name/codename, email address, encrypted password hash (Argon2 / bcrypt), and optional avatar URL.
  • Biometric Baselines: Age, height, body weight, and primary fitness objective — utilized exclusively for Mifflin-St Jeor metabolic calculations.
  • Workout Logs: Completed repetition counts, sets, exercise timestamps, and yoga pose hold durations.
  • Wearable Telemetry: Heart rate, blood oxygen (SpO2), systolic/diastolic blood pressure, and step counts if you sync a device via the Wearable Hub.

All cloud backups are synchronized via your private Firebase instance with AES-256 encryption. For offline users, telemetry resides exclusively in encrypted local browser storage.

03 Rate Limiting & Web Application Firewall (WAF)

To defend against credential stuffing, brute-force incursions, and automated scrapers, the Luminix security gateway enforces:

  • Authentication Shield: Strict sliding-window rate limit of maximum 5 attempts per 15-minute window on all authentication endpoints.
  • Universal API Limiting: 60 requests per minute ceiling on telemetry and computation endpoints.
  • Payload Size Guard: Immediate HTTP 413 rejection for request payloads exceeding 1MB (15MB for authorized media exports).
  • Threat Sanitization: Automatic dropping of payloads containing SQL injection, XSS signatures, or null bytes.

04 Cookies & Telemetry Preferences

Luminix uses minimal, privacy-respecting cookies and local storage tokens:

  • Essential Cookies: JWT authentication tokens and session security state necessary for platform login.
  • Functional Storage: User preferences (white/dark mode theme, metric/imperial units).
  • Analytics Telemetry: Optional client-side performance metrics. Telemetry is completely disabled if you select "Essential Only" in the Cookie Consent Banner or enable "Do Not Track" (DNT) in your browser.

05 Your Global Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your physical location, you retain total dominion over your biological data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: Export your full biometric history at any time via Luna AI Clinical PDF Export.
  • Right to Rectification: Update your baseline metrics instantly in the profile dashboard.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Purge your entire profile, telemetry logs, and wearable records with one click in the security settings.
  • Non-Discrimination: Platform features remain 100% accessible even if you decline non-essential cookies.
