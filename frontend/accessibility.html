♿ LUMINIX COMMITMENT TO DIGITAL INCLUSION: Luminix was engineered by Ram Charan Teja with a core belief that health intelligence, postural self-awareness, and mindfulness should be universally accessible. We actively engineer and optimize our user interface to conform with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1) Level AA.

01 Keyboard Navigation & Focus Architecture

Every primary and secondary interactive element across the Luminix sanctuary is fully operable without a mouse:

  • Tab & Shift+Tab Ordering: Logical focus progression through navigation bars, workout selectors, nutrition calculators, and profile settings.
  • Visible Focus Indicators: High-contrast vermilion focus rings (outline: 2px solid var(--vermilion)) make the currently active element clearly identifiable.
  • Modal Dismissal: The ESC key instantaneously closes any active overlay, including the Search Palette, Kyoto Share Modal, Cookie Settings, and Biometric Drawers.
  • Skip Navigation: Direct skip links and semantic landmark architecture permit immediate jumping past repetitive headers directly to primary exercise workouts.

02 Screen Reader & Semantic Hierarchy

Our templates and dynamic DOM elements are built with semantic HTML5 and Accessible Rich Internet Applications (WAI-ARIA) specifications:

  • Landmark Structure: Proper use of <header>, <nav>, <main>, <aside>, and <footer> containers for seamless screen reader jumping.
  • Explicit ARIA Labels: Every icon-only button (search, close, camera switch, social share) contains explicit aria-label and title attributes describing its exact programmatic action.
  • Descriptive Image Alternative Text: Visual assets, instructor avatars, anatomy diagrams, and UI state indicators carry rich, context-aware alt descriptions.
  • Form Labels: All input elements in the authentication views, BMI calculators, and meal planners have permanently associated <label> elements and assistive error announcements.

03 Sensory Accommodations & Motion Safety

We acknowledge sensory sensitivities and diverse physiological conditions:

  • Reduced Motion Compliance: When your operating system requests reduced motion (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce), all heavy 3D camera pan animations, WebGL transitions, and particle flows are immediately disabled or replaced with static dissolves.
  • High-Contrast Color Ratios: Text typography is rendered with curated contrast ratios meeting or exceeding the 4.5:1 ratio for normal text and 3:1 for large display headers against our deep Kyoto background.
  • No Auto-Playing Audio: Workout timers and countdown sounds never auto-play without prior user initialization. Audio alerts respect global muting toggles.

04 Alternative Biomechanical Pathways

For athletes or users who cannot or choose not to use real-time optical camera pose tracking:

  • Manual Repetition Logging: The adaptive gym engine allows full manual logging of repetition counts and hold durations without requiring camera activation.
  • Static Pose Guides: Complete step-by-step text, joint alignment cues, and breathing directions are provided for all 17 yoga asanas and gym exercises.
  • Audio Cues: Synthesized speech and tone cues announce workout state changes for users with low vision.
